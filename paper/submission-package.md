The provisional TMLR route was rechecked against the official [author guide](https://jmlr.org/tmlr/author-guide.html) on 18 October 2026. It calls for an anonymized PDF using its LaTeX template, anonymized PDF/ZIP supplements up to 100 MB, and complete author profiles/disclosures. Submissions carry CC BY 4.0 from submission; no such agreement is accepted here. A future anonymous package must avoid identifying repository links and author-bearing metadata. Existing public source approval does not satisfy those formatting requirements.

TMLR's [acceptance criteria](https://jmlr.org/tmlr/acceptance-criteria.html) emphasize supported claims and informative findings, including substantive reproducibility work. This supports keeping the paper's current reliability/reproducibility framing provisional; it does not establish that the present artifact is publishable or will be accepted.
