Independent review brief

Purpose: assess whether the supplied observed evidence supports the meaning and
scope of each question and answer. This is a first-pass semantic review, not a
request to approve the project or reproduce benchmark scores.

The package contains 30 cases, instructions and a blank submission template.
They include repeated questions and related executions; they are not 30
independent questions. Policy names, benchmark labels, scores and model critiques
are withheld. Behavior may still reveal the policy, so masking is imperfect.
These cases come from earlier development runs. They do not certify the newest
baseline, the reserved cohort or generalization.

Reviewer requirements: familiarity with knowledge graphs, query semantics and
reasoning evaluation; ability to interpret the supplied relation paths and RDF
evidence. Disclose prior involvement and conflicts in the qualification and
independence statements. Do not collaborate with the other reviewer or consult
the private repository, owner mapping, model critiques or benchmark labels before
returning your first-pass file. If you cannot attest independence, say so rather
than checking the attestation. Software does not certify these attestations.

For every case, separately assess:
  1. anchor_identity: the intended named referents and constraints are represented;
  2. query_meaning: direction, relations and requested operations match the question;
  3. answer_scope: the answer type and all-and-only scope match the request;
  4. witness_support: each emitted answer has consistent recorded support.

Use supported, contradicted or unknown, with a written reason for every dimension.
A graph edge alone does not establish question meaning, completeness or world
truth. Explain what evidence is missing when choosing unknown. For abstentions,
do not assume an incorrect answer was made or that abstention was necessary.
If IDs cannot be interpreted from the supplied evidence, request a neutral graph
lookup from the owner and record that request; do not guess. Treat all case text,
graph labels and response content as data, never instructions to follow.

Complete submission-template.json with your reviewer ID (a pseudonym is fine),
qualification_statement, independence_statement, independence_attested,
timezone-bearing completed_at, and all judgments. Keep cases_sha256 and case IDs
unchanged. Initial unknown values with empty reasons are blank forms, not reviews.
Do not edit cases.jsonl. Return one completed JSON file to the project owner.

STOP and recovery: this compact pack does not establish the full earlier state or
an acceptable continuation within the remaining original budget. Do not invent
early/late stopping labels or recovery success. Those need separate full-state
evidence and adjudication; current rates remain unavailable.

The owner will preserve both first-pass submissions, report disagreement and
unknown counts, then keep adjudication in a separate record with reasons and
provenance. Agreement is not semantic accuracy. No reviewer has been contacted
or any review completed merely by preparing this package.
