# Contribution decision — 16 October 2026

**REVISE repair/verifier design; NO-GO submission or improved-method claims.** The restricted prototype now answers real questions, but the completed comparison does not support repair benefit.

Five repeatedly used development cases give three exact answers, one partial and one abstention (F1 .6727). Ten further training-pilot questions give fixed F1 .40 versus repair .30. The sole exact-answer difference arises before repair, from nonidentical mention parsing. A follow-up holding the recorded starting prefix identical restores parity: .40 versus .40, with repair charging 3,471 additional observed tokens (+16.48%) and no additional exact recovery. This same-case follow-up is diagnostic, not ten more independent samples; its elapsed time is constructed, not fresh end-to-end timing. [Raw evidence and analysis](../results/paired-pilot-report.md).

Valid witnessed graph paths still permit semantically wrong answers: Chicago is returned through an identity relation for an attractions question; advertising characters are returned for insurance types. The same-model semantic gate accepts both. Recorded-row audits and graph presence do not resolve question meaning.

The synthetic study also shows no acquisition advantage in its fixture family. Its stopping-related expansion reduction is not real token savings, and recovery yield compares different realized costs. H1–H3 remain unestablished.

Broad relational guidance and failure-aware backtracking overlap [CoG](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.708/); relation-first exploration overlaps [CoR](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.2138/). These sources require full method/code review before any distinct-contribution claim. Current evidence supports an auditable prototype and exploratory negative-result report, not novelty or benchmark superiority.

Next: strengthen semantic obligations on development data, diagnose whether correct programs exist before spending on repair, independently adjudicate answer scope and feasible continuations, reproduce a named baseline, resolve/document snapshot differences and freeze an adequately sized evaluation. If method gains do not hold, consider a rigorous reproducibility/reliability study with useful failure lessons. Do not select a flattering subset or change the primary endpoint after test outcomes.

[Publication gates](../evaluation/publication-readiness.md) define completion evidence. Existing server supports the immediate work; no extra purchase or public submission is authorized by this decision.
