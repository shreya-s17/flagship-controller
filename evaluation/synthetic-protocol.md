# Synthetic development protocol

This development experiment checks software behavior and component interactions before the primary WebQSP and CWQ protocol. It does not supersede the saved Notion protocol or certify its hypotheses.

Fifty independent base graph instances use generator seed 17. Each has two or three declared relation obligations, a factual answer and one to five distractor branches. The query plan is an experimental input. Endpoints have explicit types and all evidence carries provenance. Conflicts are positive and negative facts for the same triple, not evaluator labels passed to the controller.

Seven paired conditions are intact evidence, one support edge removed, distractor deletion, relevant conflict injection, unrelated noise at 25% and 100%, and an incorrect entity link sharing the intended display alias. Every fifth deletion case retains a duplicate alternate witness to prevent equating deletion with unanswerability. These alternatives are an intentionally simple oracle sanity case, not realistic diverse reasoning paths.

All 16 acquisition, verifier feedback, recovery and STOP combinations receive the same envelope and hard caps of eight expansions and 32 symbolic checks. This check cap is a synthetic development deviation from the four-check model-call cap in protocol v1. No reasoning revisions, LLM calls or NLI are simulated. Retrieval returns adjacent positive and negative triples plus observed endpoint types. No cached call is rebilled. Fixed-order baselines are local component controls, not reproductions of CoR or CCoR.

The independently implemented exhaustive evaluator checks all valid finite paths and factual labels. It reports grounded yield over all conditions, coverage, unsupported answers over answered cases, factual exact match, proof recall, acquired-evidence overhead and globally enumerated minimum proof size within this fixture family. Empty denominators are NA. The loop's own verifier never supplies evaluation gold.

Intervals use 10,000 paired base-question cluster resamples, seed 17, retaining all seven conditions in each cluster. They describe variability under this generator and are exploratory. No benchmark significance, Holm-adjusted hypothesis success or population generalization is asserted.

Diagnostics separately sweep expansion caps 1, 2, 4 and 8. Verifier status-flip and parse-failure rates of 5% and 15% use three seeds 17, 29 and 43, with question-specific seeds derived by SHA-256. These faults act on checker outputs; they do not alter evaluation gold. The main deterministic experiment is not repeated three times merely to inflate sample size.

Nonterminal elapsed time includes Python controller and evidence access; tokens are exactly zero because there is no model. GPU usage is unmeasured. Heuristic confidence is not calibrated probability. Recovery after each method's first failed branch is descriptive; it is not a matched failed-answer recovery rate. Accessible proofs on abstained cases do not prove a budget-feasible continuation exists, so premature abstention remains NA pending continuation audits.

The generator deliberately includes detectable type errors and irrelevant relation noise. This favors schema-based verification and known-plan filtering. All conclusions retain that limitation.
